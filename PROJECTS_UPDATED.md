Agent Prompt: Hybrid Control — “Engineering in Motion”

You are a senior creative frontend engineer, interaction designer and industrial digital-experience specialist.

Your task is to redesign and implement the Projects / Engineering Capability experience for the Hybrid Control website as a world-class React/Vite experience.

This must NOT become a conventional corporate portfolio consisting of project cards, icon grids and generic descriptions.

The purpose is to visually demonstrate that Hybrid Control engineers real industrial infrastructure, spanning:

- Process automation
- PLC engineering
- SCADA
- Industrial networks
- Electrical systems
- Water treatment
- Energy systems
- Instrumentation
- Telemetry
- Analytics
- Reporting
- Field engineering

The visitor should leave with the impression:

«“These people engineer, connect and automate serious infrastructure.”»

---

Before changing code:

1. Inspect the complete existing React/Vite application.
2. Identify the existing component structure.
3. Identify routes and page hierarchy.
4. Identify typography.
5. Identify the Hybrid Control colour palette.
6. Identify reusable components.
7. Identify current animation libraries.

2. Creative Direction

Create an experience called:

ENGINEERING IN MOTION

Supporting statement:

Automation behind critical infrastructure.

The core visual metaphor throughout the experience is:

«A signal travelling from the physical world, through Hybrid Control's engineering, into intelligent operational control.»

This signal should appear throughout the experience as a subtle animated line/pulse.

It connects:

FIELD → COMMUNICATION → CONTROL → AUTOMATION → INTELLIGENCE → OPERATIONS

As the visitor progresses through projects and capabilities, this same visual language should evolve.

3. Design Philosophy

The website should feel like a fusion of:

- Industrial engineering
- Infrastructure
- Precision
- Modern technology
- Control systems
- Technical documentation
- Premium digital storytelling

Take conceptual inspiration from the confidence and engineering credibility of companies such as Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric, Rockwell Automation and Honeywell.

Do NOT clone their websites.

Hybrid Control should have its own visual identity.

Visual Language

Use:

- Existing Hybrid Control brand colours
- Dark charcoal / near-black environments where appropriate
- Large typography
- High-quality infrastructure photography
- Thin engineering lines
- Technical annotations
- Subtle grid systems
- Process diagrams
- Industrial schematics
- SCADA-inspired metadata
- SVG diagrams
- Animated signal paths
- Subtle glow for active equipment
- Generous negative space
- Cinematic transitions

Avoid:

- Generic SaaS cards
- Excessive rounded cards
- Bright gradients everywhere
- Generic AI imagery
- Cyberpunk aesthetics
- Fake holograms
- Circuit-board stock graphics
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Constant animation
- Decorative effects with no engineering meaning

The engineering itself should provide the visual interest.

- IntersectionObserver

for major scroll storytelling.

Framer Motion

2D engineering visualisations are preferable to unnecessary 3D.

29. Motion Philosophy

Animation must explain engineering.

Good animation:

Signal travels from sensor → PLC → SCADA.

Good animation:

Generators visually synchronise before connecting to a common bus.

Good animation:

Flow passes through a flowmeter.

Good animation:

Radio telemetry travels between remote sites.

Bad animation:

Random particles flying around because they look technological.

Every major animation should communicate something about how the engineering system operates.
